I recently defended my PhD in Brain-Computer Interfaces and Virtual Reality at the University of Lille, three years spent chasing the neural signatures of vection and cybersickness through EEG caps, VR headsets, and more Python than I'd like to admit. I'm now looking for what's next, open to research and engineering roles at the intersection of BCI, XR and AI.

French-born, US educated researcher, I work in the fields of Brain Computer Interfaces, Virtual Reality and Artificial Intelligence.
I also enjoy teaching, photography, tech and climbing.
